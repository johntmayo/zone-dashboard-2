Reimbursements for Events & Outreach

Altagether Reimbursement

To request reimbursement, fill out:

Altagether can help captains communicate with their zones and bring neighbors together. Reimbursable gatherings may include coffee meetups, framing parties, block parties, or other zone activities.

Zone gathering reimbursements

The maximum available reimbursement is $5 per person in your zone for the year. For example, a zone of 40 people may request up to $200.

Eligible expenses

  • Space or picnic-area rental
  • Snacks, drinks, ice, and basic supplies
  • Supplies for lot-clearing or framing parties

We do not reimburse

  • Gas, rideshares, or transit
  • Expenses without receipts

Altadena Rotary Block Party Grant

Altadena Rotary and Grocery Outlet offer up to $300 in gift cards for gatherings that bring people together as we rebuild.

  • Use for food, drinks, supplies, or activities.
  • Email the application to Deb Halberstadt.
  • Give at least 2 weeks’ notice.
