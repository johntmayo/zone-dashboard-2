About the community

What you're joining

Altagether uses WhatsApp to make it easier for Neighborhood Captains to connect with each other and to seek and share info. It includes just a handful of groups and you can pick and choose which you'd like to join. You can also choose to not join the WhatsApp at all, but you'll miss out on all sorts of stuff.

Captain Community

For building community and connection between captains. Share updates, socialize, find collaborators, and post about random special projects.

Quick Questions

A space for captains to ask and answer specific, practical questions related to their own or a neighbor's recovery. When responding, please be honest about your level of confidence in the answers you provide, and include links or citations whenever possible. Unanswered questions will be tracked by Altagether and routed through other channels to ensure they don't fall through the cracks.

Events & Opportunities

For sharing community events around town, special opportunities, etc.

Community Issues & Discussion

For deeper conversations about issues affecting Altadena and our work as Neighborhood Captains. Share perspectives, discuss policy and community concerns, and continue conversations that go beyond a quick factual question.
