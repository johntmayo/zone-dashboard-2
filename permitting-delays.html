Know When and How to Seek Help

County Response Goals

10

Initial plan review:

Single-family Residential Projects: 10 business days after submittal

5

Re-check review:

Single-family Residential Projects: 5 business days after resubmittal

1

Plan reviewer return calls and e-mails:

within 1 business day

2

Inspection appointment:

2 business days

When assigned a plan checker, you should have the same plan checker when you resubmit. There should be no more than one plan review and two additional reviews.

When to seek help

  • Response times exceed County targets
  • Questions for plan check corrections
