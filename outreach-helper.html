Time-sensitive topics

Send before Saturday, Nov. 7

Altadena Town Council elections

Attention Captains

In-person voting on Tuesday, Nov. 3 has ended. Neighbors can still vote on Saturday, Nov. 7, from 10 AM to 4 PM. Use the add-on only if your census tract has one candidate.

Short version

For a text, a call, or a conversation

🗳️ *Altadena Town Council elections are happening this week!* As Altadena rebuilds, it's more important than ever that our community's voice is heard. Let's show up and vote! *You can still vote in person:* * *Saturday, Nov. 7:* 10 AM–4 PM There are 13 candidates running for 8 seats on the Town Council. 👉 *Find polling locations, read candidate statements, and watch the candidate forum here:* https://altagether.github.io/atc-forum-2026/#candidates Last year, Altadena set a record with 890 ballots cast. Let's beat that this year! *The more neighbors who vote, the stronger our community's voice.*

Longer version

For email or a note you leave

Skip the subject line if you are leaving a note.

Subject: Altadena Town Council elections are this week Hi Neighbor, Altadena Town Council elections are happening this week. As Altadena rebuilds, it's more important than ever that our community's voice is heard. Let's show up and vote. You can still vote in person on Saturday, Nov. 7, from 10 AM to 4 PM. There are 13 candidates running for 8 seats on the Town Council. Find polling locations, read candidate statements, and watch the candidate forum here: https://altagether.github.io/atc-forum-2026/#candidates Last year, Altadena set a record with 890 ballots cast. Let's beat that this year. The more neighbors who vote, the stronger our community's voice. [Your Name]

Optional add-on

Only if your census tract has one candidate. Paste this after the main message.

🗳️ *Why vote when there's only one candidate?* Our census tract has just one candidate running for Altadena Town Council this year, but your participation still matters! The Town Council represents our neighborhoods in conversations with LA County and other government agencies. *Strong voter turnout sends an important message: Altadena residents are engaged, paying attention, and expect to have a voice in our community's recovery and rebuilding.* Even when a race is uncontested, showing up is a way to demonstrate that our neighborhood cares about its future!

Send before January 7, 2027

Insurance claims

Attention Captains

Neighbors have until January 7, 2027 to file an insurance claim related to the fire. Add the official filing link before you send this, and only share details you have confirmed.

Short version

For a text, a call, or a conversation

Hi [Name], this is [Your Name], your Neighborhood Captain. Insurance claims related to the fire need to be filed by January 7, 2027. If you still need to file, start here: [official link]. I’m happy to help you find the right information if you get stuck.

Longer version

For email or a note you leave

Skip the subject line if you are leaving a note.

Subject: Insurance claims are due January 7, 2027 Hi [Name], I’m [Your Name], your Neighborhood Captain. Insurance claims related to the fire need to be filed by January 7, 2027. Start here: [official link]. If you’re not sure this applies to you, use that page as the source. [Your Name] [Phone]

Initial outreach to your zone

Use this when you are reaching everybody in your zone for the first time.

Short version

For a text, a call, or a conversation

Hi, I’m [Your Name]. I live nearby and I’m a Neighborhood Captain for your area. I’m part of Altagether, which is basically a network of Altadena neighbors helping neighbors stay connected and find information and resources during recovery. I definitely don’t know the answer to everything, but if you ever have a question or need help finding something, I’m happy to try to help. [Optional: We also have a neighborhood WhatsApp / text chain / Facebook group if you’d like to join.]

For email or a note you leave

I’m part of Altagether, a network of Altadena neighbors helping neighbors stay connected and find useful information and resources during recovery. I don’t know the answer to every question, but I’m happy to help find answers or connect you with someone who might. [Optional: We also have a neighborhood WhatsApp / text chain / Facebook group if you’d like to join.] You can reach me at: [Phone] [Email] [Your Name]

What is Altagether?

Send this if a neighbor asks what Altagether is.

Short version

For a text, a call, or a conversation

Altagether is a network of people affected by the Eaton Fire who volunteer to keep their neighbors connected and informed throughout recovery. Some parts of town have a captain already, some have multiple captains working together, and some areas aren’t yet covered. You can visit altagether.org to learn more or to express your interest in becoming a Neighborhood Captain yourself!

These are starting points. Change the wording so it sounds like you.
