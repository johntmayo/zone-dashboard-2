What to Say to Your Neighbors

Not sure what to say? Keep it simple. Introduce yourself, let your neighbor know what a Neighborhood Captain does, and give them an easy way to stay connected. Copy anything below and change it however you like.

Text

Hi [Name], I’m [Your Name], one of the Neighborhood Captains for our area. I’m part of Altagether, a network of Altadena neighbors helping neighbors stay connected and find useful information and resources during recovery. I don’t know the answer to every question, but I’m always happy to help find an answer or connect you with someone who might. [Optional: We also have a neighborhood WhatsApp / text chain / Facebook group if you’d like to join.]

Email

Subject: Hello from your Neighborhood Captain Hi [Name], I’m [Your Name], one of the Neighborhood Captains for our area. I’m part of Altagether, a network of Altadena neighbors helping neighbors stay connected and find useful information and resources during recovery. My role is really just to be a local point of connection. I don’t know the answer to every question, but I’m happy to help find answers, connect you with resources, or point you toward someone who may be able to help. [Optional: We also have a neighborhood WhatsApp / text chain / Facebook group if you’d like to join.] Feel free to reach out anytime. [Your Name] [Phone / Email]

Phone or In Person

Hi, I’m [Your Name]. I live nearby and I’m one of the Neighborhood Captains for this area. I’m part of Altagether, which is basically a network of Altadena neighbors helping neighbors stay connected and find information and resources during recovery. I definitely don’t know the answer to everything, but if you ever have a question or need help finding something, I’m happy to try to help. [Optional: We also have a neighborhood WhatsApp / text chain / Facebook group if you’d like to join.]

Note or Letter

Hi Neighbor, I’m [Your Name], one of the Neighborhood Captains for our area. I wanted to introduce myself and make sure you know there’s someone nearby you can reach out to. I’m part of Altagether, a network of Altadena neighbors helping neighbors stay connected and find useful information and resources during recovery. I don’t know the answer to every question, but I’m happy to help find answers or connect you with someone who might. [Optional: We also have a neighborhood WhatsApp / text chain / Facebook group if you’d like to join.] You can reach me at: [Phone] [Email] [Your Name]

These are starting points, not scripts. Change the wording, make it sound like you, and use whatever is useful.
